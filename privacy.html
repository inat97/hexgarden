Hex Garden

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no server.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No location, no contacts, no health data. Your progress stays on your iPhone. We never receive them.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Your progress: gardens played, scores and stars, daily results and streak, unfinished gardens.
  • Your settings: haptics, daily reminder time, garden theme.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Notifications

If you allow it, one daily-garden reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Hex Garden Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Pro has been purchased.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that backup like any other app, under Apple's privacy policy. We have no copy. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete — it is all on your device and under your control. Questions are still welcome: developers9x@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. If the app ever starts sending data off your device, this page will say so first and the app will ask you.

This page describes the app as built. It is not legal advice.
