Hex Garden

Plant a garden, one hex at a time.

A calm tile-placement game. Each hex tile carries patches of flowers. Turn it, place it next to your garden, and score when matching flowers touch. Close a flower bed on every side and it blooms. A new daily garden for everyone, 40 gardens to grow through, and no ads.

How to play

  • Tap a lit spot next to your garden to place the tile in hand there. Tap it again (or use the arrows) to turn it. Press Plant when you like it. The number above the tile shows what it will score.
  • Every side where two matching flowers touch scores +2.
  • A flower bed that is closed on every side — by other tiles, stones, ponds or the garden's edge — blooms: +1 per petal and +2 per tile in the bed.
  • Later gardens add ponds (+1 for every flower edge touching water), bees (+2 for each different flower touching a bee tile), stones and butterflies (a bed with a butterfly scores its bloom twice).
  • You see the next two tiles, so you can plan. You get one undo per garden.
  • Each garden has three star targets. Earn at least one star to open the next garden.
  • The daily garden uses the same tiles for everyone that day, with one scored try. Play every day to grow your streak. Sharing your result shares only your score and stars, never the garden.

Help

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Settings › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

Refunds. Apple handles payments and refunds: request a refund from Apple.

Move or back up your progress. Everything lives only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup. There is no account or server to restore it from.
